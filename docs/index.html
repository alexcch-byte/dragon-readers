This browser demo plays the full reading interaction from the plan: sentence-in-context, drag-to-scrub phonics, revealed pictures, tricky (sight) words, a spoken help button, and a read-aloud page finish — using your browser's built-in speech voice. No accounts, no ads, no tracking. Once the page has loaded it works without internet if your device has a built-in voice.
